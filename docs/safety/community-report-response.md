# Campus community report response procedure

Use this procedure when a student reports harassment, impersonation, spam, threats, unsafe content, or misuse of CampusConnect.

## Receive and triage
1. Record a report reference, category, time received, and content location.
2. Assess immediate danger or credible threats before routine moderation.
3. Preserve only evidence necessary to assess the report; restrict access to it.
4. Avoid copying private messages, contact details, or allegations into public channels.
5. Assign a named reviewer and an expected follow-up time.

## Review and action
- Apply the published community rules consistently.
- Consider context and distinguish disagreement from targeted abuse or credible threats.
- Use the least restrictive action that adequately addresses the reported conduct.
- Require a second reviewer for serious account restrictions where practical.
- Escalate credible threats or immediate safety concerns to the designated university contact or appropriate authorities.

## Communication and appeal
- Tell the reporter that the concern was received without promising a particular outcome.
- Notify affected users when policy permits and doing so does not create additional risk.
- Explain moderation decisions and provide the available appeal route.
- Keep appeal review separate from the original decision-maker where practical.

## Closure
Record the decision, rationale, reviewer, action date, appeal status, and any follow-up. Delete temporary evidence copies when no longer needed under the approved retention policy.
