# Campus community moderation playbook

Use this playbook to respond consistently to reports involving harassment, impersonation, spam, threats, or unsafe content.

## Triage

- Capture the report ID, content location, report time, and category.
- Assess urgency and credible safety threats first.
- Preserve only the evidence needed to investigate; restrict access to it.
- Avoid publicly repeating personal information or allegations.

## Response

- Apply the published community rules consistently.
- Use the least restrictive action that adequately addresses the violation.
- Escalate credible threats, exploitation, or immediate danger to the designated institutional contact or appropriate authorities.
- Notify affected users where policy permits and provide an appeal path.

## Moderator safeguards

- Require individual moderator accounts and role-scoped access.
- Record moderation actions, reasons, and reviewer identity.
- Review moderator access periodically and remove access when duties end.
- Do not use private student information for unrelated moderation purposes.
