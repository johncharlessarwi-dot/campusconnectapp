# CampusConnect Community Safety Guidelines

CampusConnect brings students together across posts, comments, direct messages, events, marketplace listings and accommodation discovery. These guidelines define expected conduct and a practical response process.

## Community standards

Members must not:

- Harass, threaten, bully, stalk or discriminate against another person.
- Publish another person's private contact details, documents, location or images without permission.
- Impersonate a student, landlord, university employee or service provider.
- Post scams, phishing links, fake opportunities or deliberately misleading listings.
- Sell prohibited or illegal goods, or use the platform to facilitate unlawful activity.
- Share examination answers or academic work in ways that enable cheating.
- Send spam, mass unsolicited promotions or malicious files.

## Marketplace and accommodation safety

- Describe products and property accurately, including condition, price and availability.
- Do not claim a seller, landlord or property is verified unless the platform has completed the stated verification process.
- Encourage users to inspect goods or accommodation safely before payment.
- Warn users not to share passwords, one-time codes or sensitive identity documents in public posts or chats.
- Keep payment and handover decisions between the parties; platform messaging is not proof of payment or ownership.

## Reporting workflow

A report should include the relevant content or listing, a short reason and any immediate safety concern. Users should avoid reposting harmful material as evidence.

Suggested moderation workflow:

1. Receive and acknowledge the report.
2. Assess urgency, credibility and potential harm.
3. Preserve only the evidence needed for review.
4. Apply proportionate action under the platform's published rules.
5. Notify affected users where safe and appropriate.
6. Record the decision, rationale and any appeal path.

## Proportionate moderation actions

Depending on severity and repeat behaviour, actions may include a reminder, content removal, listing suspension, temporary account restriction or account removal. Credible threats or immediate danger should be escalated to appropriate local authorities or emergency support; moderators should not attempt to investigate beyond their role.

## Privacy and moderator access

- Limit report access to authorised moderators.
- Do not expose reporter identity to the reported user unless required by a lawful process.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive information.
- Keep an auditable record of moderator actions.
- Define retention and deletion periods for reports and supporting evidence.

## Appeals

Where practical, provide a way to appeal moderation decisions. A reviewer who was directly involved in the original decision should not be the sole appeal reviewer.

These guidelines should be reflected in product terms, reporting UI, moderator training and the applicable local legal review before launch.
