# Student content moderation policy

This policy supports a safe and useful CampusConnect community.

## Disallowed content
- Threats, targeted harassment, hate-based abuse, and impersonation.
- Spam, scams, phishing links, and deceptive requests for money or credentials.
- Publishing another person's private information without authorization.
- Malicious files, unsafe links, or content that facilitates harm.
- Repeated disruption of learning or campus services.

## Moderation principles
- Assess context and apply rules consistently.
- Use the least restrictive action that addresses the harm.
- Preserve a minimal record of the decision and policy reason.
- Escalate credible threats and immediate safety concerns through designated university channels.
- Do not expose reporter identities unless disclosure is authorized and necessary.

## Appeals
Provide an appeal route for users affected by significant restrictions. Where practical, have a reviewer who was not responsible for the original decision assess the appeal.

## Transparency
Publish clear community rules and explain material moderation decisions without disclosing private reports or evidence.
